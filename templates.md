# Skeletons and snippets

Generated by `npm run render` from `snippets.yaml` and the skeletons in `src/skeleton.js`: do not edit by hand.

In a skeleton's body, each `{place}` is filled with one snippet of that type. In the greeting, the snippets, the subject line and the signature, these slots are filled from the contact and its rep: `{first_name}`, `{company}`, `{when}`, `{system_of_record}`, `{ai_tool}`, `{subject}`, `{rep_name}`, `{rep_title}`. In step 1 `{subject}` is the subject snippet; in steps 2 and 3 it is that same rendered subject.

## Render checks on the copy

Word caps count the body only: the greeting and signature sit outside them.

| Step | Body word cap |
| --- | ---: |
| Step 1: first touch | 90 |
| Step 2: follow-up | 50 |
| Step 3: closing note | 30 |

Banned phrases: case-insensitive patterns no rendered email may match.

- `congrat`
- `recent news`
- `saw that you`
- `orchestration stack`
- `layoff`
- `headcount reduction`
- `restructur`
- `cut (spend|costs?)`
- `efficiency mandate`
- `cost[- ]?cut`
- `I hope this`
- `circle back`
- `synerg`
- `revolutioni[sz]e`
- `right person`
- `someone else`
- `point me`
- `who (else|should I)`

No email may name a Humaans customer: Lovable, Poolside, Fyxer AI, Tandem Health, Quantexa, Sellpy, Manychat, Gigs, Croud, Threecolts, Healf.
